Is your solar doing its job?

Check your rooftop system against your own bill. Free, and your bills and kWh stay in this browser.

You need: your latest electric bill, and the solar kWh for the same dates from your inverter app.

About SCND

Your inverter app shows how much your panels made. Your bill shows what you paid. SCND lines the two up for your roof and tells you whether the system is performing or whether there is something to ask your installer.

  • Solar performance

    Compares each month's output with what your roof should make, and tells a cloudy month from a faulty system.

  • Consumption split

    Shows how much solar your home used and how much went to the grid.

  • Net-metering bill

    Walks through your bill: what each kWh sent to the grid earns you, and why fixed charges keep the total above zero.

  • Degradation

    Compares the year-over-year drop in output with the loss your panel warranty allows.

Also: a weather check for low months, savings and payback against what your installer promised, battery health for hybrid systems, and a one-page findings sheet to hand your installer.

The three steps below take you through it: your system details once, then a bill with your inverter kWh, then the answer, which prints as a one-page list of questions for your installer.

Privacy and limits

Your data

  • Your system details, bills and inverter kWh stay in this browser, and SCND's server never receives them. The one exception is below. SCND keeps no accounts, database or cookies.
  • "Check the weather" sends only your location, rounded to 0.1° (about 11 km), and a date range to Open-Meteo and NASA POWER, and only when you tap it.
  • Your Auto, Light or Dark choice is kept in this browser's local storage. If you tick "Keep my data on this device", your setup and bills are kept there too, in this browser only, until you untick it. "Save" writes a file to your own device without uploading it, and so does "Remind me next month", a calendar file that holds only the reminder text and a date.
  • So SCND opens without a signal, your browser keeps a copy of this site's own files (pages, scripts, fonts, sun data). Your bills are never part of it.
  • The site is served by Cloudflare, which sees your IP address to deliver the page, like any web host. SCND runs no server code of its own, so nothing of SCND's sees, logs or stores it.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and basic technical details such as country, browser type and page load time. It uses no cookies and does not follow you across sites. SCND sends it nothing you type. Cloudflare's own privacy policy applies to those counts.
  • Feedback goes through a Google Form. Answers are stored in Google Forms and read only by Alfred Balaga, who runs SCND as a personal project. Name and email are optional. For a data question, or to have a feedback answer deleted, use "Give feedback" and include the name or email you gave, or email contact@alfredbalaga.com. The same address takes security reports (security.txt).
  • The Ko-fi link and the tip QR are plain links and a picture from this site. Nothing is sent to Ko-fi unless you open its page, and then Ko-fi's own privacy policy applies.

Limits

SCND estimates from your own figures and public sun data, and sun data can be off by 5 to 10% for one place and month. Each result is a screening check that gives you questions for your installer. It does not replace an engineering inspection or a financial or legal opinion, and it judges no installer or product.

Before acting on warranty or money matters, talk to your installer or a qualified engineer.
