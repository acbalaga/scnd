# SCND audit, 13 Nov 2026

Requested by Fred: audit security, functionality and the maths, propose improvements (visitor value, Cloudflare Workers Free plan, open audit gaps), and file ranked tickets. The tickets are GitHub issues #37 to #55. They are written to be executed one at a time by another agent. Nothing in the app was changed by this audit.

## Method

- Read end to end: `src/worker.js`, `src/headers.js`, `wrangler.jsonc`, `public/_headers`, every module in `public/js/`, the tests, `docs/SPEC.md`, `docs/NEXT.md`, `docs/pre-launch-audit.md`.
- `node --test`: 145 of 145 pass.
- Live data, 13 Nov 2026: NASA POWER daily and Open-Meteo satellite daily (2020-01-01 to 2026-11-11) for Manila, Laoag, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo and Baguio, run through `calc.weatherBaseline` and `calc.weatherIndexPeriod`. Public city points only.
- Headless Chromium at 390 px against a local static server, CPU throttled 4x, for input latency.
- Cloudflare Workers docs (limits, static assets billing, `_headers`), read 13 Nov 2026.

**Not checked:** the live site and its headers, DNS, the Cloudflare dashboard, a real phone. The 2 Oct audit covered the live site. Its open dashboard and device items are carried into #52.

## Verdict

The calculation core is sound where it was checked: geometry, energy balance, savings, the bill decoder and the edge cases all behave as the SPEC says. No secret, no injection path and no employer data turned up. Three things need attention before more promotion:

1. The site can be taken offline for a day by anyone, for free (#37).
2. PR reads low for Metro Manila in the dry season by more than the app says (#38).
3. The app becomes unusable as an owner's history grows (#39).

## Checked and sound

- **Geometry and transposition.** Tilt 0 returns GHI within 0.04%. Incidence angle matches Duffie and Beckman. Leap years and bills that cross months and years split correctly.
- **Energy balance.** On-grid and hybrid splits agree with `hybridBalance`. Battery losses are not counted as solar used.
- **Money.** Savings are the avoided purchase plus the export credit, capped at the variable charges, with the uncounted credit named. No tariff is hard-coded.
- **Period mismatch between the two baselines.** The weather index uses a 2020 to 2025 baseline and the typical year is 2001 to 2020. Outside Manila the two agree within 2.3% on the annual mean (Laoag 1.023, Cebu 0.989, Davao 0.978, Iloilo 1.010, Baguio 1.018), so this is not a separate finding.
- **Source agreement.** Where both sources exist (2024 to 2025), the mean gap between the two weather indices is 2 to 3 points (5 at Iloilo), and 4 to 25% of months exceed the 5-point "uncertain" limit. The limit is doing its job.
- **Save files.** `parse()` copies known fields only, rejects text in number fields, bad dates and wrong versions.
- **XSS.** Two `x-html` sinks, both fed numbers and validated dates. Free text goes through `x-text`.
- **Outbound links.** All carry `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- **Third parties.** No request leaves the origin until "Check the weather", apart from the disclosed Cloudflare beacon.
