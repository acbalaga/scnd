What is SCND?

Your inverter app shows how much your panels made. Your bill shows what you paid. SCND lines the two up for your roof and tells you whether the system is performing or whether there is something to ask your installer.

  • Solar performance

    Compares each month's output with what your roof should make, and tells a cloudy month from a faulty system.

  • Consumption split

    Shows how much solar your home used and how much went to the grid.

  • Net-metering bill

    Walks through your bill: what each kWh sent to the grid earns you, and why fixed charges keep the total above zero.

  • Degradation

    Compares the year-over-year drop in output with the loss your panel warranty allows.
