Which irradiance source can SCND trust?

Each source's 2020 to 2023 average for each calendar month, five PH cities, compared against the Global Solar Atlas long-term monthly GHI (Solargis satellite model; PVGIS SARAH-3 does not cover the Philippines). Wet season = Jun to Oct. Pass rule: wet-season bias within ±10% in every city. A 4-year mean against a long-term mean carries a few percent of year-to-year noise, so read ±5% as agreement.

Bias vs Global Solar Atlas by city (wet / dry season)

Bias = source mean ÷ GSA mean − 1 over the calendar months in that season. MAPE in brackets = mean absolute error across those calendar months.
