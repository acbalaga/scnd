Read this location with care. Sun here is very clear from February to April, and the data source tends to read low in the dry months. Treat the dry-season expected yield as a range, not a single number.
DC/AC above 1.3. Your panels are much bigger than your inverter. On sunny days the inverter caps output, so a lower PR in bright months can be normal.
