What performance ratio (PR) means

PR compares what your panels made with what panels their size would make in the sun they got, if nothing were lost. It is the fairest single number for "is my system doing its job?", because it takes the weather out.

How it is worked out

PR = kWh made ÷ (panel size in kWp × sun on the panels in kWh per m²)

A 5 kWp system that got 150 kWh/m² of sun in a month would make 750 kWh with no losses. If it made 600 kWh, its PR is 600 ÷ 750 = 0.80.

Heat, dust, wiring and the inverter always take some, so real systems sit below 1.00. PR is not panel efficiency (about 20% for modern panels). It measures the losses after the panel rating.

What is healthy

  • 0.75 or more: healthy. SCND expects this from a working home system.
  • 0.65 to 0.75 (June to October) or 0.62 to 0.75 (November to May): a bit low, worth watching. The dry-season floor is lower because the sun data SCND uses reads a little low in those months.
  • Below that: low.
  • Above 0.90 or below 0.30: SCND says "check numbers". That usually means the dates, the kWh or the panel size were entered wrong, or the system was off for part of the period.

Philippine installers publish 78 to 82% as a benchmark for commercial rooftops. Homes often sit a little lower because of shade and heat.

Why one month proves nothing

The sun on your roof in a given month is an estimate from satellite data, and it can be off by 5 to 10% for one place and month. A cloudy month also reads low against a typical year. That is why SCND raises a flag only after two low months in a row, and why it offers a weather check that compares your bill dates with the sun that actually fell.

What pulls PR down

  • Shade from a neighbour's wall, a water tank or a tree, often only part of the day.
  • Dust, bird droppings and leaves, worst late in the dry season.
  • Heat: panels make less when hot.
  • An inverter much smaller than the panels, which caps output on sunny days.
  • Brownouts: on-grid inverters switch off when the grid goes down.
  • Faults: a tripped breaker, a failed string or an inverter error.

SCND works out PR for each bill from your inverter kWh, your panel size and the sun at your location.
