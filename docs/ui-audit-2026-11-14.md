# SCND UI and UX audit, 14 Nov 2026

Requested by Fred: audit the interface against accepted UI and UX standards and close the gaps, without adding load time or changing the site's identity. His own observation: the lower part of the page and the footer look busy.

## Method

- Headless Chromium against a local static server: 390 px (phone) and 1280 px (desktop), light and dark, in five states (landing, setup form, on-grid example, hybrid example with 14 bills, findings sheet open).
- Measured in the page: tap target sizes, text contrast against the real background, font sizes, heading order, horizontal overflow.
- Benchmarks: WCAG 2.2 AA, Nielsen's ten usability heuristics, the Gestalt grouping principles (proximity, common region), and the 44 px touch target from Apple's HIG and WCAG 2.5.5.

**Not checked:** a real phone, a screen reader session, real first-time owners. The numbers below are from an emulated viewport.

## What already passes

| Check | Standard | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text contrast, light and dark | WCAG 1.4.3 (4.5:1) | No failures on screen. |
| Tap targets | WCAG 2.5.8 (24 px), HIG (44 px) | Every button, summary and standalone link is 44 px or taller. Links inside a sentence are 17 px, which the inline exception allows. |
| Reflow | WCAG 1.4.10 | No horizontal scroll at 390 px. |
| Focus ring, reduced motion, one h1, labelled inputs, field errors in words | WCAG 2.4.7, 2.3.3, 1.3.1, 3.3.1 | In place. |
| Destructive action asks first | Nielsen 5, error prevention | "Remove" confirms in words. |
| Load | no build step, self-hosted fonts, no third-party script | Unchanged by this audit: no new file, no new request. |

## Gaps found and closed

| # | Finding | Standard | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Footer was six unrelated blocks in one column: three links at three weights, guides wrapping ragged over three lines, a long data credit, the feedback caveat, a fold, the sign-off. Nothing marked where the app ended and the footer began. | Gestalt proximity and common region; Nielsen 8, minimalist design | A rule above the footer. Three actions at one weight. Guides on an even grid under a small label. Fine print in two folds. One short credit line with both source links in view; NASA's full acknowledgement is in the "Data sources and credits" fold. |
| 2 | "Please leave bill and account numbers out of feedback" sat four blocks below the "Give feedback" link it belongs to. | Gestalt proximity; Nielsen 5 | Directly under the link. |
| 3 | Between the bills and the totals sat a weather card, two loose buttons, a save prompt card and a keep-on-device card. "Add another bill" was a card away from the list it adds to, and the findings sheet opened three cards below its button. | Gestalt proximity; Nielsen 4, consistency; Hick's law | Order is now bills, "Add another bill", weather, totals under an "Across your bills" heading, then one "Next steps" card (findings sheet, save and reminder, keep on device) right above where the sheet opens. |
| 4 | Bill cards (h3) hung straight off the h1, and the totals had no heading. | WCAG 1.3.1, 2.4.6 | "Your bills" (screen readers) and "Across your bills" (visible) h2s. |
| 5 | On a wide screen the chart labels drew at about 20 px against 14 px body text, because the SVG scales with the card. | Visual hierarchy; Nielsen 4 | Labels hold at about 14 px from 560 px up. |
| 6 | With a long history the payback chart's month labels ran together ("JunJulAug"). | WCAG 1.4.12 intent; legibility | At most three month labels between the end dates, evenly spread. |
| 7 | Three fold markers in use: "+", a browser triangle (chart tables, tip QR), "Edit/Hide". | Nielsen 4, consistency | Chart tables and the tip QR use the same "+" and "−". "Edit/Hide" stays, because it says what the fold does. |
| 8 | On the landing page the only start button was a full phone screen above the end of "What you get". | Fitts's law; standard landing practice | "Check my system" repeats at the end of the explainer, landing only. |
| 9 | A link-style button that wrapped to two lines centred its text while sitting on the left edge. | Alignment | Left aligned. |

## Left for Fred

- **Figures in Chivo Mono.** Thousands separators are a full cell wide, so "₱10,800" reads slightly as "₱10 , 800" at stat size. The face is a decided part of the identity, so it is unchanged. `font-feature-settings` or a tighter letter-spacing on `.stat b` would be the smallest fix.
- **The step rail looks tappable but is not.** Making steps 1 and 2 jump to their sections would match what the shape promises. It is a behaviour change, so it is not done here.
- **Help text density.** Most cards carry two or three 14 px grey paragraphs. Each one answers a real question, and trimming them is a copy decision.
- **Open-Meteo credit.** The visible line still reads "Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0)", and the wording is still to be checked against open-meteo.com/en/licence (carried over from the earlier note).
